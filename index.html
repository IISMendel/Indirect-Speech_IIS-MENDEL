Direct & Indirect Speech

Discorso diretto e indiretto — Grammar & Practice

Quando riportiamo le parole di qualcuno usiamo il discorso indiretto (Reported / Indirect Speech). Verbi, pronomi ed espressioni di tempo e luogo subiscono un backshift o si adattano al nuovo contesto.

A Say vs Tell

SAY

  • ➤ Non richiede oggetto persona: "She said she was tired."
  • ➤ Può avere to + persona: "She said to me she was tired."
  • ➤ Usato nel discorso diretto: "I'm tired," she said.

TELL

  • ➤ Richiede sempre l'oggetto persona: "She told me she was tired."
  • ➤ Mai tell to: ✗ She told to me…
  • ➤ tell sb, told sb, told sb to do sth
"I can't come tomorrow," he said.
→ He said he couldn't come the next day.
"I can't come tomorrow," he said to Anna.
→ He told Anna he couldn't come the next day.

C Reporting Questions

Yes/No Questions

Usiamo if o whether. Ordine affermativo (no inversione, no do/does/did).

"Are you coming?" she asked.
→ She asked (me) if / whether I was coming.

Wh- Questions

Il pronome interrogativo diventa congiunzione subordinante. Ordine affermativo + backshift.

"Where do you live?" he asked.
→ He asked (me) where I lived.

⚠ Attenzione: nessun punto interrogativo nelle domande riportate (sono frasi subordinate).
✗ She asked where did I live?  |  ✓ She asked where I lived.

Riportare le risposte

I verbi introduttivi più comuni per le risposte sono answer e reply. Le risposte brevi usano solo l'ausiliare (con backshift).

"Is she sleeping?" "Yes, she is."
→ He asked if she was sleeping and I answered she was.
"Have you ever been to Africa?" "No, I haven't."
→ He asked if I had ever been to Africa and I answered I hadn't.

F Conditionals, Wish, Had Better & Would Rather

Periodi ipotetici nel discorso indiretto

TipoDiscorso direttoDiscorso indiretto
Tipo 0 (sempre vero)If you push this button, it starts.She said that if you push this button, it starts. (invariato)
Tipo 1 → diventa Tipo 2If she phones, I will be happy.He said that if she phoned, he would be happy.
Tipo 2 (invariato)If she phoned, I would be happy.He said that if she phoned, he would be happy.
Tipo 3 (invariato)If she had phoned, I would have been happy.He said that if she had phoned, he would have been happy.

Forme invariate nel discorso indiretto

FormaDiscorso direttoDiscorso indiretto
had better'You'd better stay at home.'→ He said I'd better stay at home.
would rather'I'd rather wake up early.'→ He said he'd rather wake up early.
wish + Past Simple 'I wish I were rich.' → He said he wished he were rich.
wish + Past Perfect 'I wish I had seen her.' → He said he wished he had seen her.

Regola chiave: had better, would rather e i modali già al passato (should, could, might, would, ought to, needn't) non subiscono backshift nel discorso indiretto.

Quick Reference

Verbi di riporto principali

say, tell, ask, add, admit, advise, agree, announce, claim, complain, confess, deny, explain, insist, offer, promise, refuse, remind, reply, suggest, warn

Strutture con to-infinitive

advise, agree, ask, decide, expect, hope, offer, promise, refuse, want, warn (sb) + to-inf.

Strutture con -ing

admit, deny, recommend, suggest + -ing form
